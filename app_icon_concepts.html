App icon and name options

General gamepad options in the Console palette, not tied to cricket. Each icon is shown large, at launcher size, and as a round icon, then on real-looking home screens.

1. Face buttons

The four coloured controller buttons. Instantly reads as "gamepad" and stays crisp at any size.

2. D-pad

A bold white D-pad with a green "connected" light in the middle. The simplest and most graphic.

3. Wireless stick

A joystick sending a signal, which says what the app does: a wireless controller for your PC.

4. Phone as controller

A phone held sideways with a stick and buttons on it: the most literal. Details get small on the home screen.

Name options

PadLinkSays what it does: your phone linked to your PC as a pad.
ThumbpadYou play with your thumbs; friendly and easy to remember.
CouchpadPlay your PC games from the sofa.
PocketPadA controller that's always in your pocket.

All four fit under an icon without being cut off. Before publishing, search Google Play for the one you pick to make sure no other controller app already uses it.
