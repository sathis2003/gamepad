PocketPad privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

What PocketPad collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. It does not read your address book, location, storage or any identifier of your phone.

Network use

The app sends controller inputs (button presses and joystick positions) to the PC address you type in. That traffic stays on your local network between your phone and your PC. No input data is sent to us or to any third party.

Permissions

Internet: required to reach the PC on your local network. Vibration: used for button feedback and can be turned off in the app.

The website

This site is static and sets no cookies. If you enter your email in the updates form, it is stored by Formspree and used only to send PocketPad release news. Reply to any email to be removed.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/sathis2003/pocketpad-releases.
