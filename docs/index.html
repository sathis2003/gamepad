Your phone is the controller.

PocketPad turns an Android phone into an Xbox controller for any Windows game. No account, no ads, free.

Download for AndroidAPK, Android 8 and up Download for WindowsPocketPad Server, Windows 10 and 11

You need both: the app on the phone, the server on the PC.

Set up in three steps

  1. Run PocketPad Server on the PC

    Windows asks for admin once: the server opens port 8080 in the firewall and installs the controller driver. SmartScreen may say "unrecognised app"; choose More info, then Run anyway. Keep its window open while you play.

  2. Install the app on the phone

    Open the APK. Android asks to allow installs from your browser; allow it once.

  3. Open the app and tap your PC

    Phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi. Your PC shows up in the app by name; tap it and play. Windows sees an Xbox 360 controller, so any game with controller support works. Up to four phones can join the same PC.

Lower latency

Wi-Fi through a home router is about 5 to 15 ms. Taking the router out of the path gets it under 4 ms.

Phone hotspot
Turn on the phone's hotspot and connect the PC to it. Type the hotspot address shown in the server window.
USB cable
Plug the phone in and turn on USB tethering in Android settings. Fastest and steadiest, no Wi-Fi needed.
